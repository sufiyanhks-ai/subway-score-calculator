How Scoring Works in Subway Surfers

Subway Surfers uses a layered scoring system that rewards players who run farther, collect more coins, and maintain consistency across missions. Understanding it gives you a real advantage.

The Multiplier Is Everything

Your score multiplier is the single most powerful factor in your final score. At 1x, running 100,000 meters gives you 100,000 points. At 10x, the same run gives you 1,000,000. This is why veteran players always focus on maxing their multiplier before chasing high scores. You build it by completing mission cards and weekly challenges — not by playing longer sessions.

Coins vs Score: What Is the Difference?

Coins and score are two separate currencies. Coins are collected mid-run and used to buy characters, hoverboards, and upgrades in the shop. Your score reflects how far you ran, multiplied by your current multiplier, plus bonuses. A coin magnet power-up does not directly inflate your score — but it increases your coin haul, which adds a coin bonus at the end of the run.

Hoverboard Effect on Score

A hoverboard does not add points directly, but it extends your run by absorbing one crash. A longer run means more distance, more coins, and a significantly higher base score. This is especially impactful when your multiplier is high. For a full breakdown of which hoverboards give the best advantage, see the complete Hoverboard Guide on SubsurfApps.com.

Tips To Maximize Your Score

These six strategies will consistently push your score higher regardless of which board or character you use.

⚡

Prioritize Multiplier Over Everything

Complete mission cards as fast as possible. Each card set increases your multiplier. A player at 15x will always outscore a player at 5x, even if the 5x player runs twice as far.

🛹

Use Hoverboards on Long Runs

Save hoverboards for runs where your multiplier is maxed and you want to push distance. Read the hoverboard guide to pick the right board for each situation.

🧲

Activate Coin Magnet Early

Use the coin magnet at the start of your run when coins are densest. The end-of-run coin bonus compounds with your base score for a meaningful total boost.

📅

Complete Weekly Hunts

Weekly hunts are the fastest way to earn mission card XP and push your multiplier higher. Set a reminder and clear them before they reset every Sunday.

🎯

Focus on One Character

Upgrading one character fully gives better score bonuses than spreading upgrades thin across many. Pick a favourite and max them out first.

🎁

Open Mystery Boxes Mid-Run

Mystery boxes can grant power-ups that activate immediately, extending your run and adding bonus points. Do not skip them when they appear on the track.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the Subway Surfers score calculated? ▼
Your score is calculated by multiplying the distance you run by your score multiplier. Coins, mystery box bonuses, and hoverboard usage add extra points on top of the base score. This calculator estimates your total based on all those combined inputs.
How do I increase my score multiplier in Subway Surfers? ▼
You increase your multiplier by completing mission card sets. Each completed set gives you +1 to your multiplier. Weekly hunts and special events also reward mission XP, making them the fastest way to level up your multiplier.
Does using a hoverboard increase your score? ▼
Yes, indirectly. A hoverboard absorbs one crash and keeps your run alive. More distance covered means a higher base score, which then gets multiplied. The longer your run, the bigger the impact. See the full hoverboard guide on SubsurfApps.com for details on which boards last longest.
What is the highest possible score multiplier? ▼
The maximum score multiplier in Subway Surfers is 30x, achievable by completing all available mission card sets. Reaching 30x takes time but completely transforms your scores — a 30x player scoring 500K base will hit 15 million points on the same run a 5x player would score 2.5 million.
